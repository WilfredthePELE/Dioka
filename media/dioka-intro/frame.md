# Dioka introduction
A single white-studio portrait loop of a stylish mature European shoemaker working on a cognac leather shoe. Pure white canvas. No captions, decorative copy, logos, sound or extra scenes are rendered into the film. The responsive website overlays only DIOKA in emerald #075b42 using its existing Cormorant wordmark. The tools, hands, shoe, leather, linen and workbench provide the foreground detail; the explicit minimalist brief overrides extra decorative/metadata density. A slow, seamless camera breath over six seconds. This is an animated generated portrait, not live-action hand movement.
